Jewelry Image Review

Your review stays with you.

Effective October 25, 2026 · version 1.0.0

Jewelry Image Review by Jewels Retouch processes the local images you choose in your browser tab. It does not upload images, make API requests, collect analytics, create accounts or send your review to Jewels Retouch.

What is held in memory

The open tab holds your selected images, their file names, types, sizes and dimensions, checklist selections and notes. The extension does not use browser storage, cookies, databases or remote servers. Reset or close the tab to discard the review. Reset and image replacement release the image object URLs. Browser memory management may reclaim the underlying memory later.

Your downloads

When you choose Download review JSON, your browser saves a file containing image metadata, checklist choices, notes and a timestamp. It contains no image pixels. That file remains on your device until you delete it and may contain sensitive file names or notes. Your browser may also retain normal download history or file-picker history under its own settings.

Links you choose to open

Jewels Retouch links open the website in a separate tab only when you activate them. Website services have their own privacy terms. Visiting them is optional and is not needed to use this tool.

Manual judgment

The checklist records your selections. It performs no AI analysis, automatic change detection or accuracy certification.
