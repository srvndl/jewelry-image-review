# Answer: preserve this specific item

A catalog image should represent the item offered. For used or vintage jewelry, retain condition evidence buyers need.

---

# Choose source views

Keep original overall and detail photographs. Record visible marks, wear, and unique features before styling.

---

# Separate image jobs

Use a clean product view for identification and a styled scene for presentation. Keep both linked to the same stock identifier.

---

# Review identity and condition

Check that edits have not removed meaningful wear, changed stones, or replaced distinctive details. Compare to the actual item where possible.

---

# Prepare destination exports

Check crop and background against each destination. Verify that linked detail views still show the same item and approved version.

---
