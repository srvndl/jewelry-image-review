# Answer: count and trace before approving

Inspect visible stone count, arrangement, setting, and outline beside the source. Attractive sparkle is not evidence of preserved construction.

---

# Map visible evidence

Mark which stones, prongs, and engraved areas are actually visible. Label hidden or blurred details unverified.

---

# Count and compare stones

Check center and accent stones, spacing, color appearance, and arrangement. Stop when count or layout changes.

---

# Trace the setting

Follow prongs, bezel edges, gallery openings, links, and closure. Check whether highlights conceal breaks or invented connections.

---

# Choose the next action

A changed critical feature means reject this output. Use a different treatment, manual finishing, or new capture if evidence is inadequate.
