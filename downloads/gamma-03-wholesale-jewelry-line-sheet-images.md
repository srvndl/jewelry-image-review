# Answer: organize around the buyer selection

Use approved product views tied to exact model codes. A useful line sheet makes a requested range easy to compare.

---

# Freeze the selection

Record the buyer request, selected model codes, and document version. Confirm which items and sets belong in the sheet.

---

# Normalize presentation

Use comparable crops and backgrounds. Include verified dimensions when relative size matters; do not infer scale from a generated image.

---

# Attach current commercial facts

Pull prices, availability, and order information from your current business records. Image styling is not a source for product facts.

---

# Review before sending

Confirm every selected code has its approved image. Check omissions, duplicate codes, and superseded exports. Retain the sent selection version.
