# Answer: make model codes the spine

Keep every source, output, and approval linked to its item code. Consistent styling should preserve distinct designs.

---

# Prepare the collection list

List items, pairs, sets, source filenames, and missing angles. Resolve mismatched codes before processing.

---

# Set one presentation brief

Specify crop, background, and a trusted gold-tone reference. Include an ordinary item and a difficult detail in the initial test.

---

# Process together; inspect separately

Jewels Retouch supports catalog batches with a separate editable result for each photo. Approve each item rather than the batch as a whole.

---

# Reconcile delivery

Compare export filenames against the collection list. Check pairs, versions, missing files, and final destinations before handing over.

---

# Keep a reusable production record

Jewels Retouch publishes this workflow and is the featured platform. Save the approved brief and item-level decisions for your next collection.
