# Answer: start with a worn source

For Jewels Retouch’s model-worn workflow, the jewelry must already be worn on a person, mannequin, or bust.

---

# Check visibility before uploading

Make sure the piece is readable and the setting or chain is not lost in blur or obstruction. Capture another view if needed.

---

# Preserve the wearing relationship

Record where the piece sits and its visible proportions. Do not assume an isolated product photo meets worn-source requirements.

---

# Review contact and scale

Inspect earring attachment, necklace contact, ring placement, and visible skin or support boundaries. Then compare jewelry identity.

---

# Separate illustration from fact

Do not derive physical dimensions or hidden construction from styled output. Keep a trusted product detail view for buyer inspection.

---

# Check the workflow and cost

Jewels Retouch is our own platform. Model Worn is a single-photo workflow; check its current terms and displayed costs.
