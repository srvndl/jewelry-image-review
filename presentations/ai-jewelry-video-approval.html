Part 1 of 6

Answer: approve the complete clip

A good first frame cannot approve a video. Review identity throughout motion and transitions.

Approve and identify the source

Use an approved source image. Record its version, intended campaign, and any reference footage you are authorized to provide.

Part 3 of 6

Pass one: normal playback

Watch the whole clip for composition, campaign suitability, abrupt changes, and distracting motion. Record timestamps needing inspection.

Part 4 of 6

Pass two: pause on detail

Check stones, prongs, links, outline, and reflections at flagged moments and transitions. Inspect any newly revealed surface.

Pass three: verify product implications

Compare to source and actual piece where available. Reject invented mechanisms or construction; review a revised render again from the beginning.
